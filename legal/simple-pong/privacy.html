Privacy Policy

Applies to: the Simple Pong! app on Android (Google Play package com.LittleFriars.SimplePong, published under LittleFriars) and these legal pages.

Simple Pong! is an arcade Pong game with single-player and local two-player modes on the same screen. We do not run any servers for Simple Pong! and we do not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal data ourselves. However, the app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show interstitial ads between matches, which collects certain device and ad-related data as explained below.

1. Who we are

Simple Pong! is developed and published by Alexandre Valente (under the developer name LittleFriars).

  • Email (privacy and support): alexmfv1@gmail.com
  • Established in: United Arab Emirates

2. Data we collect directly: none

We do not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No accounts or sign-in: You never provide a name, email address, phone number, username, or password.
  • No developer servers: All gameplay—both single-player against the AI and local two-player on the same device—runs entirely on your phone or tablet.
  • No sensitive permissions: The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or files. It only uses standard network permissions required to load advertisements.

3. Data stored locally on your device

To remember your preferences and your best rally score between sessions, Simple Pong! saves a few values locally on your device only:

WhatWhyWhere it stays
Game preferences (sound on/off, number of sets: 1, 3, or 5, and preferred paddle side: left or right)So you don't have to reconfigure your options every time you open the game.On your device (PlayerPrefs).
Best hit counter (playerInfo.dat)To display your highest consecutive paddle hit count (Best) during a match.On your device (app local storage).

This information is never sent to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling Simple Pong!.

4. Third-party services and advertising

While we do not collect data ourselves, Simple Pong! is built with the Unity game engine, distributed through Google Play, and integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display interstitial advertisements after a match ends.

ServiceWhat it doesWhat data it may collect
Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)Loads and displays interstitial ads when a match finishes.Device and network information such as your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), Android Advertising ID, device model, operating system version, app interaction/ad performance data (such as ad views and clicks), and diagnostic information.
Google Play ServicesDistributes and updates the app, and provides standard platform diagnostics.Installation, crash, and diagnostic data governed by Google's privacy policy and your device's diagnostics settings.

Google processes data collected through AdMob and Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Managing advertising preferences

You can limit or reset your Android Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in your device's settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your Android version).

5. When you contact us

If you email us at alexmfv1@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include in your message so that we can reply to you. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail). We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your inquiry and up to 2 years afterwards.

6. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions or concerns about your child's use of Simple Pong!, please contact us at alexmfv1@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about players on our own servers, we do not have an account or profile tied to you. You can:

  • Delete local game data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Control ad identifiers through your Android device's Google Ads settings, or exercise your privacy rights regarding Google's processing directly with Google under their Privacy Policy.
  • Ask us to delete support emails you have sent us by writing to alexmfv1@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If we update Simple Pong! in a way that changes how data is handled (for example, if we remove ads or add new features), we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

Questions or requests about this policy: alexmfv1@gmail.com.
