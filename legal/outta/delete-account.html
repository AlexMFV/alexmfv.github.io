Delete your account

App: OUTTA (Google Play package com.alexmfv.outta)
Developer: Alexandre Valente
Contact: alexmfv1@gmail.com

This page explains how to delete your OUTTA account and the data linked to it, in the app or without it, what we delete, what we keep, and for how long.

1. Delete it in the app

  1. Open OUTTA and go to Me → Privacy & safety.
  2. Under Your account, tap Delete your account.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. In the "Delete your account?" box, tap Delete. (Keep it closes the box and changes nothing.)

If your account is paused, the "Your account is paused" page also has a Delete your account button. A paused account can still delete itself.

2. Delete it without the app

If you can't use the app (you uninstalled it, lost your phone, or can't sign in), email us:

  • To: alexmfv1@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete my OUTTA account
  • Include:
    • the phone number on your account, with its country code (for example +971 50 123 4567);
    • the first name you use on OUTTA;
    • whether you want the account deleted straight away, or with the same 30-day grace as in the app (section 4). If you don't say, we use the 30-day grace.

How we check it's you. Your phone number is the only thing that identifies your account, and we don't hold an email address for you. Before deleting anything, we reply to ask you to confirm details that only the account holder is likely to know, such as your first name on OUTTA and roughly when you joined or which clubs you belong to. If we can't reasonably confirm the request comes from the account holder, we ask you to sign in with that number and delete the account in the app instead.

How long it takes. We act on email requests within 7 days of confirming it's you, and email you to say it's been done. From then on, it works just like deleting in the app (sections 3 to 6): you're hidden at once, and the account is removed for good after 30 days, or straight away if you asked for that. So the longest it takes is 37 days from when we confirm it's you.

We never ask for your SMS code, and we will never ask you to send a password (OUTTA doesn't use passwords).

3. What happens straight away

As soon as you delete your account:

  • you're signed out on every phone;
  • other members stop seeing you: you leave "Who's going" lists, hosts' member lists and your Welcome Circle, and your chat messages show as "Former member";
  • your booked spots and waitlist places are given up, so the next person on the waitlist moves up;
  • your Welcome Circle seat is given back, and you leave your Circle;
  • clubs you host close: their future sessions are called off (everyone's bookings are cancelled) and the clubs are archived.

4. The 30-day grace, and how to cancel it

You have 30 days to change your mind. To cancel the deletion, sign in again with the same phone number within 30 days. You'll see "Welcome back. Your account is staying."

What comes back: your account, your profile and your answers. What doesn't come back: the spots, waitlist places, Welcome Circle and hosted clubs you gave up when you deleted.

If you asked by email and want to cancel, reply to our confirmation email within the 30 days, or sign in again with the same number. A deletion done straight away can't be cancelled.

5. What we delete after 30 days

After the 30 days, a daily job permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it:

  • your phone number and sign-in record;
  • your first name and profile;
  • your onboarding answers: city, neighbourhood, interests, vibe, life stage, age band and free times, and your agreement to the Community rules;
  • your club memberships, bookings and waitlist places;
  • your Welcome Circle sign-ups, seats, RSVPs and Circle records;
  • people you blocked and people who blocked you, and any host stops;
  • your chat read markers and any custom activities you created;
  • clubs you host, with their sessions, bookings and chats;
  • the short "joined" and "first time" notes the app posted in chats about you.

Accounts that asked for a sign-in code but never finished setting up are deleted the same way 30 days after sign-up, without notice.

7. Using the guest preview

If you only used "Look around without a number" in the beta, there is no account to delete: the preview runs on sample data kept on your phone, and nothing is sent to OUTTA's server. To remove it, tap Reset demo in the app, or uninstall OUTTA.

Uninstalling the app on its own does not delete a real account. Use the steps in section 1 or 2.
