## 7. Meeting people in person

OUTTA is about meeting people offline. That brings real-world risks, and you need to take care of yourself.

- **You're responsible for your own safety** when you meet people you found through OUTTA, at a club session, a Welcome Circle meetup or anywhere else.
- **We don't vet members or hosts.** We don't check identity, background or criminal records. Hosts are members like you. Clubs are published as soon as a host creates them, without review. We don't inspect venues or meeting points, and we don't run the activities.
- **We aren't there.** We don't supervise sessions or meetups, and what happens at them is between the people who take part, within the law.
- **A few habits that help:**
  - Meet in public places, especially the first time.
  - Tell a friend where you're going and when you'll be back.
  - Get there and home your own way.
  - Keep your phone number and home address to yourself until you're sure.
  - Know your limits for the activity: a run, a hike or a padel game is your call.
  - Leave whenever you feel uncomfortable. You don't owe anyone an explanation.
  - **If you're in danger now, call 999.** Then tell us what happened through "Report something that happened" in the app.

## 8. If you host a club

Any member can run a club in Host mode. If you do, you also agree to these points:

- **Be accurate.** Your club's name, description, activity, level, meeting point, map pin and session times must be true and kept up to date.
- **The place.** Only choose a meeting point that is open to your group, and that you have the right to use. Respect the venue's own rules. If the activity or place needs a permit, booking or licence, getting it is your job.
- **Your house rules.** You can set house rules for your club. They must be fair, lawful and in line with our Community guidelines, and you must apply them in good faith.
- **Nothing unlawful.** Don't organise anything that breaks the laws of the UAE, or anything unsafe for the people who come.
- **Look after your members.** Plan activities that suit the level you advertise, and tell members in the club chat if plans change.
- **Members' details.** As a host you see the first names of your members, when they joined and how often they've come, and the first names of people booked on your sessions. Use them only to run your club. Don't copy them elsewhere or contact people outside OUTTA without their agreement.
- **No charging through OUTTA.** Sessions on OUTTA are free during the beta. Don't use OUTTA to collect money from members.
- **You're responsible for your club.** You run your sessions, not OUTTA. You may be responsible to your members and to others for what happens at them.
- You can stop a member from booking your clubs ("Stop booking") if they break your house rules or the guidelines. Use it fairly.
- If you delete your account, your clubs close: their future sessions are called off and booked spots are cancelled.

## 11. Moderation, pausing and closing accounts

**What we can do.** If we believe someone has broken these terms or the Community guidelines, or that it's needed to protect people or comply with the law, we can:

- remove a message (it then shows "Removed by OUTTA" in the chat);
- reset a first name;
- remove a club, or remove someone from a club or a Welcome Circle;
- **pause** an account for a set time;
- **close** an account for good (a pause with no end).

**How it works.** Reports are read by a person. We aim to look at every report, usually within a day, but we can't promise a time. When three different people in a chat report the same message, it is hidden from everyone but its author until we look at it. A paused account can't write, chat, join or book, and its future bookings are cancelled. A paused member can still see and cancel their own bookings, and can delete their account. If an account is paused when it's deleted, we keep a protected, one-way code made from its phone number (never the number itself) until the pause would have ended, for no longer than 2 years, so a new account with that number starts paused too.

**Telling you.** The person reported isn't told who reported them, and the person who reported isn't told the outcome. If your account is paused, the app tells you when you open it.

**Disagree?** Write to us at **alexmfv1@gmail.com**. We'll look again. There's no in-app appeal yet.

You have 30 days to change your mind by signing in again with the same number; after that, the account is deleted. See [Delete your account](delete-account.html) and our [Privacy policy](privacy.html) for what's removed and what's kept.
