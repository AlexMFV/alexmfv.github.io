# OUTTA Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 November 2026
**Applies to:** the OUTTA app on Android (Google Play package `com.alexmfv.outta`), including the closed beta and its guest preview, and these legal pages.

OUTTA helps adults who are new to Abu Dhabi find clubs, book a spot at sessions, chat with the people they'll meet, and join a Welcome Circle. To do that we need a little information about you. This page says what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We've tried to keep it short and plain. If anything is unclear, write to us.

---

## 2. What we collect and why

### Your account

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **Your phone number** | You sign in with it and a one-time code. We also use a confirmed number to make sure each Welcome Circle seat goes to a real person. |
| **The one-time code** | To check the number is yours. It's short-lived and never logged. |
| **An account ID and dates** (when you signed up and finished onboarding) | To link your data to your account, and to show "Joined" on your own profile. |
| **Your first name** (the name you choose, up to 50 characters) | So the people you meet know what to call you. You can change it in Edit profile. |
| **Sign-in tokens** | To keep you signed in. On your phone they're kept only in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore). |

Your account is created as soon as you ask for a code, before you type it in. If you never finish setting up, we delete it automatically after 30 days.

We **don't** ask for an email address or a password to use the app, and we don't collect a photo, your date of birth, your gender or any ID document.

### Your onboarding answers

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **City** (Abu Dhabi for now) | To show you local clubs. |
| **Neighbourhood** (optional, picked from a list) | To show what's near you and to group your Welcome Circle by area. We only ever know a neighbourhood, never your address. |
| **Interests** | To suggest clubs and to match your Welcome Circle. |
| **Vibe** (cosy, both or anything) | We store it but don't use it for anything yet. |
| **Life stage** (for example "just moved" or "studying") | To match your Welcome Circle. |
| **Age band** (18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45+) | You choose it yourself, and it's required. We use it to match your Welcome Circle with people around your age and to keep OUTTA for adults. |
| **Free times** (a weekly grid) | To match your Welcome Circle to times you can make. |
| **Your agreement to the Community rules** (which version, and when) | You need to agree before you can write anything. |

You can change most of these (neighbourhood, interests, vibe, life stage, age band and free times) under **Me → Availability & interests**. Your age band can be changed but not removed, because it's needed to use OUTTA.

### What you do in the app

- **Clubs you join**, and when you joined.
- **Bookings and waitlist places** for sessions, and when you made them.
- **Your "Maybe" or "Can't" answers** to a session pinned in a chat.
- **Your Welcome Circle**: the seat you took, whether you asked to hear when one opens near you, your weekly "going / can't" and your answers to the weekly questions.
- **Messages** you send in club and Circle group chats, and which messages you've read (for unread counts).
- **Clubs you host**: name, description, activity, neighbourhood, meeting point, the spot you place on the map (see "Your location" below), house rules, level and sessions.
- **Safety actions**: people you block, members you stop from booking your clubs, and reports you make.

We use all of this to run the service you asked for: showing your clubs, holding your spot, running your chats and your Circle, and letting you host.

### If you're reported or moderated

If someone reports you, or we act under the Community rules, we also hold:

- the report and the evidence copied with it. For a report about a person, that includes your first name and your latest 20 messages in chats you share with the person who reported you;
- any pause on your account, with its end date;
- a lock on your first name, if we reset it.

These records are kept as set out in section 7.

### Matching your Welcome Circle

We match Circles automatically, using your neighbourhood, interests, life stage, age band and free times. Matching only decides which group of six you join; it has no legal or financial effect on you. If you'd rather not be matched this way, simply don't take a Circle seat. Clubs work without it.

### Your location

- **Your phone's location** is asked for only when you tap the map's locate button, after we explain why, and only while you're using the app. We use it on your phone to centre the map on you and work out distances. **OUTTA's servers don't receive or store it**, and we don't show it to anyone, with one exception: if you host a club and tap **Use this spot** after centring the map on yourself, that position becomes your club's meeting point and is stored like any other meeting point (see "If you host a club" below).
- **Members are never shown on the map.** Pins are clubs only.
- **If you host a club**, you place its meeting point on a map. We store that exact point. Other members see the exact spot only if you choose to share it before booking. Otherwise they see the middle of a grid square about 1 km wide, which is never the real place. The exact meeting point text goes only to members with a confirmed booking, and to you.
- **Map images** come straight from OpenFreeMap (see section 5), which means OpenFreeMap sees your IP address and the part of the map you're looking at. If you centred the map on yourself, that area is around you.

### On your phone

Besides your sign-in tokens, the app saves your theme and language. If you delete your account, it also saves the date your account will be removed, so it can tell you on the Welcome screen. It never saves your number or name there. Android backup is switched off for OUTTA, so none of this is copied to a backup.

### Permissions we ask for

Internet and location (only when you tap locate). Nothing else: no camera, photos, contacts, microphone or notifications.

### When you write to us

If you email us (for a request, a report, an appeal or beta feedback), we keep your email address and what you wrote so we can answer you and keep a record of what we did. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail). We keep these emails for up to 2 years after we last hear from you.

### If you're a beta tester

To let Google Play offer you the closed beta, we keep a list of testers' Google account email addresses in the Play Console. We use it only for the beta and delete it when the beta ends.

## 3. Our legal reasons

We only use your data when the law allows it. Under the GDPR and the PDPL, our reasons are:

- **To provide the service you signed up for** (performing our contract with you): your account, number, first name, onboarding answers, bookings, clubs, chats and Welcome Circle. Without these, OUTTA can't work.
- **Our legitimate interests in keeping OUTTA safe and working**, which we've weighed against your rights: the Community rules agreement, the word filter, reports, blocks, host stops, moderation, account pauses, limits on sign-in attempts and messages, and keeping safety records after an account is deleted (section 7). The PDPL also allows processing without consent where it's needed to protect people or for legal claims.
- **Your consent**: for your phone's location, which you give through Android's permission prompt and can take back at any time in your phone's settings.
- **The law**: when we must keep or share data to comply with a legal obligation or a valid order from a court or authority.

We **don't** sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles to target you.

## 4. Who can see what in the app

| What | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Your first name** | People in your club and Circle chats, people holding a spot at the same session as you ("Who's going"), the hosts of clubs you join, and anyone who opens a club you host. |
| **Your phone number** | Only you. Other members never see it. |
| **Your age band, life stage, free times, interests, neighbourhood and vibe** | Only you. Other members never see these. (One exception: if someone reports a message in a Circle chat, our moderator sees the reported member's age band, to help spot under-18s.) |
| **Clubs you've joined** | You and that club's host (your first name, when you joined and how many sessions you've come to). Other members can't see your clubs. |
| **Your bookings** | You; the host of that session; and people holding a spot at the same session, who see your first name and initial. Waiting lists show a count only, never who's on them. |
| **Your messages** | Everyone in that chat. In a club chat, a member sees messages from the day they joined; the host sees all of them. |
| **Your Circle** | Its members see each other's first names, weekly RSVPs and answers. The café is shown only to the Circle's members, after matching. |
| **Clubs you host** | Signed-in members see the club's details. For the meeting point, see section 2, "Your location". |
| **People you block, members you stop, reports you make** | Only you. Our moderator can also see blocks and stops when reviewing a report, and sees the reports themselves. Nobody is told you blocked or reported them. A member you stop from booking sees that your club isn't taking their booking, without a reason. |

There are no public profiles and no one-to-one messages. Chats are text only: no photos, files or voice.

Messages are protected in transit but are **not end-to-end encrypted**. As the person who runs OUTTA, Alexandre has technical access to the database, including messages. We only read messages when they've been reported, or when we need to for safety or to comply with the law.

## 5. Services we use

We share data only with the services we need to run OUTTA. Each one uses it only to provide its service.

| Service | What it does for us | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Hosts our database, sign-in and live chat | Everything in section 2 that's stored on our servers, plus the technical details of your requests to it (such as your IP address). |
| **OpenFreeMap** (tiles.openfreemap.org) | Provides the map images and map fonts | Your IP address and the area of the map you're viewing. If you centred the map on yourself, that area is around you. OpenFreeMap says it has no user accounts and sets no cookies. |
| **Google Play** | Distributes the app | Google's own data about installing apps (such as your Google account), under Google's privacy policy. For beta testers, we hold the tester list described in section 2. |
| **Google (Gmail)** | Hosts our email | Your email address and anything you write to us. |
| **GitHub** | Hosts this website | Like any web host, it sees visitors' IP addresses. |
| **Google Fonts** | Provides the fonts on this website | Your IP address when your browser loads the page. |
| **Apple Maps or Google Maps** | Opens a Circle café's location, only if you tap "Open in Maps" | Whatever that app or website collects, under its own policy. |

**Text messages.** In the beta, sign-in works only with test numbers, so no SMS provider receives your number. When we add an SMS provider, we'll name it here first.

**Crash reports.** OUTTA itself doesn't collect crash reports yet. Google Play may share crash and freeze reports with us, without your name or number, if you've allowed your phone to send usage and diagnostics data. When we add our own crash reporting, we'll update this policy first, and it won't include your name, number or messages.

We'll also share data if the law requires it, for example with UAE authorities under a valid order, or to protect someone from serious harm.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **While your account is open**, we keep your account data. Messages are kept as long as the chat exists; a club's chat is deleted with the club.
- **When you delete your account** (section 8), we hide you from other members straight away and remove your account for good after **30 days**. A daily job removes it: your number, profile, answers, memberships, bookings, blocks, host stops, Circle data, any pause or name lock, the clubs you host (with their sessions and chats) and your "joined" notes in chats.
- **Accounts that never finish setting up** are deleted 30 days after the code was asked for.
- **What we keep after deletion**, why, and for how long:
  - **Your messages in group chats** stay, signed "Former member". Nothing links them to your account any more, but what you wrote stays, so other people's conversations still make sense. They're kept while that chat exists. If you want specific messages deleted, email us and tell us which ones.
  - **Reports you made** stay without your name, so we can still act on them: up to **2 years** after the report is closed.
  - **Reports about you**, and the evidence saved with any report (including copies of messages), stay to keep people safe: up to **2 years** after the report is closed.
  - **Our audit log** of moderation and account actions (account IDs and actions, never your number): up to **2 years**.
  - **If your account was paused when it was deleted**, we keep a one-way scrambled code (a hash) made from your phone number, never the number itself, until the pause would have ended, and for **2 years at most**. If someone signs up again with that number, the new account starts paused. The daily job deletes these codes when they expire.
  - We remove reports, evidence and audit-log entries by hand when they reach their 2-year limit.
- **Backups**: deleted data can stay in our hosting provider's backups for the limited period our hosting provider keeps them, until they're overwritten. We never use a backup to bring back a deleted account.

## 8. Your rights, and how to use them

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

- **See your data** (access) and **get a copy** in a common format (portability). There's no download button in the app yet. Until there is, email alexmfv1@gmail.com with the phone number you signed up with, and we'll send you a copy.
- **Correct it.** You can change your first name in Edit profile, and most of your answers under Me → Availability & interests. For anything else, write to us.
- **Delete it.** In the app: **Me → Privacy & safety → Delete your account**. You'll be signed out everywhere at once, and the account is removed after 30 days. If you change your mind, sign in again with the same number within those 30 days and your account stays (your bookings, waitlist places, Circle and clubs don't come back). Can't use the app? See our [account deletion page](https://alexmfv.github.io/legal/outta/delete-account.html).
- **Object** to how we use your data for our legitimate interests, or **ask us to limit** it.
- **Withdraw consent** for location at any time in your phone's settings. The map still works without it.

To protect your account, we may ask you to prove the phone number is yours before we act. We'll reply to every request within **30 days**.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please talk to us first. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UAE, the UAE Data Office; in the EU or UK, the authority where you live or work.

## 9. How we protect your data

- Our database checks, row by row, who may read each piece of data, and refuses everything else by default. Safety and moderation data sits in areas no app can reach.
- Data travels to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Your sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage and expire after an hour (they renew while you use the app). You can sign out of your other phones from the app.
- We limit how often codes can be asked for and how fast messages can be sent, to stop abuse.
- The app's logs hide phone numbers, names, messages, locations and keys, and they stay on your phone.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll act quickly and tell you and the authorities where the law requires.
