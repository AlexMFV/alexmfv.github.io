# OUTTA Community guidelines

**Effective 7 November 2026 · Rules version 1**

OUTTA is how people here meet in real life. These rules keep that safe.

Everyone agrees to them before writing anything on OUTTA: a message, a Circle answer or a club page. They apply in the app and at every session and meetup you find through it. They're part of our [Terms of use](terms.html).

---

## The rules

### You're 18 or over.
OUTTA is only for adults. If you're under 18, you can't use it. If you think someone here is under 18, report them.

More on how we keep children safe: [Child safety](child-safety.html).

### Respect people.
No threats or violence. No harassment, in the app or at a meetup. No hate about who someone is.

### Keep it clean.
No sexual content, in chats, club pages or your name.

Anything that sexualises or exploits a child means the account is closed for good and reported. See [Child safety](child-safety.html).

### Be real.
No scams, no spam, and no pretending to be someone else.

### What we do about it.
We don't tolerate any of this. We can remove what you wrote, remove a club, or pause or close your account. If you see it, report it: we look at every report, usually within a day.

---

## A bit more on each

**Respect people** also means taking "no" for an answer. If someone doesn't reply, doesn't want to meet again or leaves a club, let it be. Don't ask for someone's number, address or social accounts if they haven't offered them.

**Keep it clean** covers everything you write: your first name, messages, Circle answers and your club's page, house rules included. A filter checks your first name, messages, Circle answers, club names, descriptions, meeting points and custom activity names before they're saved. If it stops you, you'll see "That has a word we don't allow here. Change it and send again." Nothing is sent until you change it.

**Be real** means your own first name, your own phone number and one account. Clubs should be real clubs, with real times and places. No selling, advertising or recruiting for something else in chats.

**If you host a club**, keep its details true and up to date, choose places your group can use, keep your house rules fair, and don't organise anything unlawful or unsafe. More in section 8 of the [Terms](terms.html).

## Meeting up safely

- Meet in public places, especially the first time.
- Tell a friend where you're going.
- Get there and home your own way.
- Keep your number and address to yourself until you're sure.
- Leave whenever you want to. You don't need a reason.
- **If you're in danger now, call 999.**

We don't check members' or hosts' identities, and we aren't at the meetups. Look out for yourself and for each other.

## Reporting

If something's wrong, tell us. It takes two taps.

**What you can report:** a message, a person, a club, or something that happened (in person, too). You don't need to name anyone to report something that happened.

**Where:**
- **A message:** press and hold it.
- **A person:** their face in your Circle, or in Who's going, or in Blocked people. Hosts can also report from their member list.
- **A club:** "Report this club" on the club's page.
- **Something that happened:** Me → Privacy & safety → Safety & help → "Report something that happened".

**Reasons:** Threats or violence · Harassment · Sexual content · Hate · Scam or spam · Under 18 · Something else. You can add details (up to 500 characters). For "Something else" and "Something that happened", we ask you to tell us what happened.

**What happens next:**
- A person reads every report. We aim to look at it usually within a day, but it isn't always possible.
- When you report, we keep a copy of what you reported, so it still counts if it's later changed or deleted. For a message, that includes the 20 messages before it.
- **The person you report isn't told** that you reported them.
- You stop seeing a message you reported.
- When three different people in a chat report the same message, it's hidden from everyone but its author ("Hidden for review") until we look at it.
- We can't tell you the outcome yet. If you need to follow up, write to us at **alexmfv1@gmail.com**.
- Each member can make up to 10 reports a day.

Please only report in good faith. Making reports you know are false is itself against these rules.

## Blocking

If you don't want to see someone, block them. It's quiet: **they aren't told.**

When you block someone:
- you won't see each other's faces in Who's going or member lists;
- you won't see their messages in the chats you share ("Hidden: you blocked this person");
- you won't be put in the same new Welcome Circle.

Be aware:
- **they can still read your messages** in chats you're both in;
- they can still book and turn up at the same session;
- if they run a club you're in, you'll still see their name there and they can still see you in its lists. Leave that club to stop that;
- if you're already in the same Welcome Circle, you both stay in it, so you may still meet. If you don't want to, tell us in Safety & help.

You can block up to 200 people, and unblock anyone under Blocked people. If someone makes you feel unsafe, block **and** report them, so we can act.

## If you host: stopping a member

Hosts can stop a member from booking their clubs ("Stop X booking"). That member can no longer join or book that host's clubs, leaves its chats and loses any waitlist places. Spots they've already confirmed stay. They see "This club isn't taking your booking", with no reason given. Use it fairly, for people who break your house rules or these guidelines.

---

## What happens if the rules are broken

Depending on what happened, we can:

- **remove a message.** It shows "Removed by OUTTA" in the chat;
- **reset a first name** that breaks the rules;
- **remove a club**, or remove someone from a club or a Welcome Circle;
- **pause an account** for a set time. A paused account can't write, chat, join or book, and its future bookings are cancelled;
- **close an account** for good.

We look at what happened, not just at the number of reports. Some things, like threats, sexual content or anyone under 18, can lead to closing an account straight away. Where the law requires it, or someone is in danger, we share information with the authorities. Child sexual abuse material, or a child at risk, is always reported, as set out on our [Child safety](child-safety.html) page.

If your account is paused, the app tells you when you open it. You can still sign out or delete your account. If you think we got it wrong, write to **alexmfv1@gmail.com** and we'll look again.

---

## When the rules change

If we change these rules, everyone is asked to agree to the new version before they can write again. Reporting and blocking always work, even before you agree.
