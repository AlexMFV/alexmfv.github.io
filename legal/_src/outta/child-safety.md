# Child safety standards

**App:** OUTTA (Google Play package `com.alexmfv.outta`)
**Developer:** Alexandre Valente
**Child-safety contact:** Alexandre Valente, [alexmfv1@gmail.com](mailto:alexmfv1@gmail.com)
**Effective:** 7 November 2026

OUTTA helps adults who are new to Abu Dhabi find clubs and meet people in real life. These are our standards for keeping children safe, and what we do when something goes wrong.

---

## 1. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

OUTTA prohibits **child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)** of any kind, and **child sexual abuse material (CSAM)**. This includes:

- sharing, asking for, or linking to sexual content involving anyone under 18, including written descriptions;
- grooming: contacting, befriending or gaining the trust of a minor in order to sexually abuse or exploit them, online or in person;
- sexualising minors in messages, names, club pages or anywhere else in the app;
- sextortion, trafficking, or arranging to meet a minor for sexual purposes;
- using a club, session or Welcome Circle meetup to get access to children.

Breaking this standard means immediate removal of the content and the permanent closure of the account, and we report it as described in section 5. A closed account can't be used again: it is paused with no end date, and if it is deleted, a new account with the same phone number starts paused for up to 2 years.

## 2. OUTTA is for adults only (18+)

- Everyone on OUTTA must be **18 or over**. The Community rules every member agrees to before writing anything say: "OUTTA is only for adults. If you're under 18, you can't use it. If you think someone here is under 18, report them."
- Every member declares an age band to finish setting up; the youngest band is 18–24, and there is no option under 18.
- On Google Play, OUTTA's target audience is 18 and over.

We don't check ID, so someone could lie about their age. That's why "Under 18" is a report reason (section 4).

**If we find that a member is under 18**, we pause the account at once, close it for good, and then delete its data by hand, keeping only the reports and evidence described in section 5. They may not use OUTTA again until they are 18.

## 3. How OUTTA is built to limit risk

- **No images, video or files.** OUTTA is text only. Members can't upload or send photos, videos, voice notes or files anywhere in the app, and the app does not ask for camera or photo access.
- **No private one-to-one messages.** Chats are group chats only: a club's chat (its members and host) and a Welcome Circle's chat (up to six people).
- **No public profiles, and members are never on the map.** Other members see a first name, never a photo, phone number, age or address.
- **A word filter** checks messages, first names, club names and descriptions, meeting points and custom activity names before they are saved, and refuses words we don't allow.
- **Three reports hide a message.** When three different members of a chat report the same message, it is hidden from everyone but its author until we look at it.

## 4. How to report

**In the app** (two taps, and the person you report is not told):

- **A message:** long-press it and choose **Report**.
- **A person:** from their face in your Circle, from "Who's going" on a session, or from Blocked people. Hosts can also report from their club's member list.
- **A club:** tap **Report this club** on the club's page.
- **Something that happened in person**, with or without a name: **Me → Privacy & safety → Safety & help → Report something that happened**.

Choose **Under 18** if you think someone is a minor, or **Sexual content** for sexual messages or behaviour, and add any details you can.

**Block:** you can also block anyone. They aren't told, and you stop seeing their messages.

**Outside the app:** email [alexmfv1@gmail.com](mailto:alexmfv1@gmail.com). You don't need an OUTTA account to report.

**If a child is in danger now**, call the police on **999** in the UAE, or your local emergency number. Don't wait for us.

## 5. What we do with a report

- **A person reads every report.** We look at reports about children first.
- **We keep the evidence.** When a report is made, OUTTA copies what was reported (for example the message and the 20 messages before it) so it can't be lost by editing or deleting. Reports and evidence are kept for up to 2 years after the report is closed, and for longer if the law or the authorities require it.
- **We act on it.** Depending on what we find, we remove the content, remove a club, take someone out of a club or Circle, and pause or permanently close the account. For CSAE, the account is closed permanently.

**When we find or are told about CSAM, or a child at risk**, we:

1. remove the content at once, so no one else can see it;
2. preserve the evidence: the report and the copies saved with it, kept out of reach of every member;
3. report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org), and to the police in the United Arab Emirates, or the police in the country concerned;
4. close the account for good;
5. cooperate with any lawful request from the authorities.

OUTTA has no formal agreement with any of these bodies, and this page does not claim one.

## 6. Following the law

OUTTA complies with the child-safety laws that apply to it, including the laws of the United Arab Emirates, where OUTTA operates.
